# AUREON — a bioluminescent abyss

*Maximum graphics quality, in AUREON's OWN style — organic, dark, neon-in-black. NOT gothic, NOT architectural, nothing lifted from VEILFIRE/TIDEGLASS/KINDLE. Those set the craft bar (swarm/BAR.md); the look is our own.*

## Imagine it

You drift in a deep-sea void, near-black, far below any sun. The only light is
LIVING light: vast translucent **jellyfish** glowing from within, their bells
pulsing soft cyan and violet; **coral** mounds tipped with points of biolume;
drifting clouds of **luminous plankton** like slow sparks. Thin, ghostly
**god-rays** fall from a surface impossibly far overhead, catching the motes.
The water itself is a medium — deep teal fading to black, faint **caustics**
rippling on the seabed. Weightless, alive, otherworldly.

## Signature effects (AUREON's own)
- **Subsurface scattering** — the translucent glowing creatures (the hero).
- **Volumetric particulates** — plankton + god-rays + deep fog.
- **Refraction** — light bending through the bells and rising bubbles.
- Emissive biolume + physical bloom, ACES grade, deep blacks.
