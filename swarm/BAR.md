# AUREON quality bar — match the CRAFT of TIDEGLASS / VEILFIRE / KINDLE, not their look

These three of Galen's worlds set the **quality** bar only — the level of lighting,
atmosphere, material richness, and photographic grade AUREON must reach. They are
**NOT a style to copy.** AUREON has its OWN distinct graphic style. Match the craft,
never the aesthetic.

**Hard rule: do NOT hardcode any of their looks anywhere** — no gothic arches /
cloister / cathedral forms lifted from VEILFIRE, no sunset-ocean from TIDEGLASS,
no smoke-fire from KINDLE. Those are references for *how good it should look*, not
*what it should look like*.

## The craft level to reach (style-agnostic)
- **Atmosphere** — a real graded sky/environment, aerial depth, not a flat backdrop.
- **Lighting** — soft shadows, ambient occlusion, a bounce of GI; forms read in light.
- **Materials** — surfaces that read as a substance (procedural PBR), not flat colour.
- **A signature optical effect** — reflection/refraction, caustics, volumetrics, or
  subsurface — used boldly (each reference leans on one; AUREON picks its own).
- **Emissive + physical bloom**, deep blacks, **ACES tonemap**, warm/considered grade
  so the frame reads as a photograph, not a render.

## The rule
When AUREON's first frame lands, probe it and judge it *beside* these three for
**craft** — is the light as considered, the atmosphere as deep, the grade as filmic?
Any dimension below their level → lift that node. But the **style** is AUREON's own,
whatever we choose — measured on quality, never on resemblance.
